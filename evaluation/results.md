Generated by `evaluation/score.py` from the marked sheets and the unblinding keys. Do not
edit by hand; re-run the script. Rules: `evaluation/queries.md` § 3, clarified blind in § 7.

- **Judge:** verdicts proposed by Claude (Claude Sonnet 5.5, claude.ai) from the card text and
  adopted unchanged by the developer; blind to the system, not to the project. No human
  validation yet. See `evaluation/provenance/` and `queries.md` § 10 (disclosed 2026-10-13).
- **Profile for B:** synthetic `category_scores` {'technology': 3, 'writing': 2, 'design': -1}, `CATEGORY_BOOST` = 0.02. C is the same with the boost at 0.
- **precision@5** = relevant listings in the top 5 ÷ 5, always 5 (C5). A duplicate
  vacancy counts once per list (C3; 6 declared pairs).
  It changed 11 of the per-query scores below: all-sources Q08 A, all-sources Q08 B, all-sources Q08 C, all-sources Q10 B, all-sources Q10 C, Adzuna-only Q08 A, Adzuna-only Q08 B, Adzuna-only Q08 C, Adzuna-only Q10 A, Adzuna-only Q10 B, Adzuna-only Q10 C.
- **Significance:** two-sided exact sign test over non-tied queries. With this few
  queries it can detect only a large, consistent effect; a high p is not evidence of no effect.
