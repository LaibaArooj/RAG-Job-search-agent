Quiz me on: $ARGUMENTS

If the argument is "weak", read `docs/LEARNING_LOG.md` and pick the three lowest-rated Tier 1
rows instead.

Rules:

1. Ask **one question at a time**. Wait for my answer before the next one. Never show me the
   answer before I've attempted it.
2. Start with "explain X in your own words", then move to the follow-ups an examiner would
   actually ask — the *why*, the alternatives, the weaknesses.
3. Mark honestly. If my answer is vague, hand-wavy, or uses a term correctly without showing I
   understand it, say so and ask me to try again. Do not be encouraging about a bad answer —
   that costs me marks later.
4. If I get it wrong twice, explain it properly, then re-ask a variant of the same question a
   few questions later to check it stuck.
5. Five questions per session.

At the end, give me:
- A rating for each topic covered on the log's scale (🔴 / 🟡 / 🟢 / ⭐)
- The specific gaps to go and close
- Then update the relevant rows in `docs/LEARNING_LOG.md` with the ratings and today's date
